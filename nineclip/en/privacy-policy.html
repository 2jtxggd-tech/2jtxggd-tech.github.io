NineClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy explains how NineClip stores and handles data.

1. Overview

NineClip is a macOS app that stores frequently used text on your Mac and copies it to the clipboard from the menu bar.

2. Information Stored on Your Mac

To provide its features, NineClip stores the following information on your Mac:

  • Group names, search names, and tags
  • Snippet names, body text, and notes
  • Fixed slots and additional slots
  • Today Palette
  • History of recently used items
  • Settings such as theme, visible item count, shortcuts, and launch at login

This information is stored in NineClip’s App Sandbox container.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

NineClip does not include account registration, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

NineClip does not automatically send your snippets, settings, or history of recently used items to servers operated by the developer or any third party.

4. Clipboard

When you select a snippet, NineClip writes its body text to the macOS system clipboard.

NineClip does not control other apps or paste text automatically. It does not use Accessibility permission to send keystrokes.

5. Import, Export, and Backup

NineClip imports, exports, backs up, and restores snippets only through files you explicitly select in the macOS file picker.

NineClip does not upload these files over the network. If you select a cloud-synced folder as the destination, syncing is governed by that service and your macOS settings.

6. Contacting Support

If you email the support address shared by multiple apps, the developer receives your email address, message, and any files you attach.

The NineClip app does not automatically send support inquiries. Information received is used only to respond to your inquiry, retained only for as long as needed to provide support, and deleted when it is no longer needed.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

NineClip does not provide your data to third parties for advertising, marketing, tracking, or data sales.

Support emails are sent and received through the email services used by you and the developer, and each service’s privacy policy applies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This page will be updated if NineClip’s features or data handling practices change.
