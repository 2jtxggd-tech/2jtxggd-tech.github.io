About NineClip

NineClip is a snippet app that lets you retrieve frequently used text from the Mac menu bar and copy it to the clipboard.

NineClip is copy-only. It does not control other apps or paste text automatically. Your snippets, settings, and history of recently used items are stored on your Mac.
